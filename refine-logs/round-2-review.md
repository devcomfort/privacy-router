# Round 2 Review

**Verdict:** REVISE
**Scores:** Problem 8; Novelty 6; Mechanism clarity 8; Experiment credibility 6; Feasibility 6; Overall 6.

## Strongest Remaining Rejection

The joint privacy–utility claim was not credibly powered at effective N=300. A paired binary non-inferiority margin of 3 percentage points would generally require more independent clusters unless task-success discordance were exceptionally low. The estimand needed to be defined per base task, and leakage superiority plus task-success non-inferiority needed simultaneous confidence bounds.

Hydration was also an unmetered disclosure path. Any cleartext release after placeholder hydration had to become a policy-mediated, counted egress event with recipient/invocation binding and replay resistance. For cumulative recall, the 5% FPR threshold needed to be selected solely on an independent calibration split.

## Revision Triggered

- Added a discarded pilot and pre-test sample-size gate.
- Defined paired task-level leakage and utility estimands.
- Required all co-primary privacy and utility bounds to pass.
- Separated calibration from test thresholding.
- Initially made hydration a first-class egress, then removed external hydration entirely in the final simplification.
